## Aircraft

20th century aircraft *'What if?' scenarios.

### Douglas F-7 Super Skylancer

What if Heinemann threw out the original F5D Skylancer design, and entertained a J79 powered F5D-2 (redesignated F-7 from 1962) to best both the Vought F8U Crusader (later F-8) and Grumman F11F-1F Super Tiger as a single engine low-cost, radar equipped fighter on Essex class carriers, to the dual engine F4H (later F-4) from super carriers? What would that do to the F-8? How might it have impacted export sales? The F-104 Starfighter in Europe? Even the Israeli's in 1967 when France cut them off from the Mirage V? Even the Argentine Air Force and Top Gun in the 1980s?
