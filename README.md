What would that do to the F-8? How might it have done in export sales? Versus even the F-104 Starfighter in Europe? Even the Israeli's in 1967 when France cut them off from the Mirage V? And how might that affect other history, even the Argentine Air Force and the US Navy's Top Gun in the 1980s?

### 1976 DSAA Directive 5105.38-M

In October 1976, a joint GAO investigation and Senate oversight hearing exposed predatory U.S. Air Force Foreign Military Sales practices, prompting the Department of Defense to issue DSAA Directive 5105.38-M to halt hidden research and development waivers and enforce strict lifecycle affordability audits. This Two-Tier FMS Directive permanently segmented global aerospace exports by officially cataloging Northrop’s single-engine F-19 Tigershark as the standard Tier-2 point-defense interceptor while reserving heavy multirole platforms like the General Dynamics F-16E/F and supersonic LTV A-7F conversions for Tier-1 and specialized maritime strike markets. Consequently, this regulatory overhaul fostered a highly competitive three-platform export ecosystem that bypassed restrictive presidential export bans, drove down allied defense budget strains, and established long-term propulsion dominance for General Electric.

* [Northrop_F-19_Tigershark](aircraft/Northrop_F-19_Tigershark) - Utilizing the existing tooling for the F-5, the Tigershark was the 3rd generation, N-156 based, primary export fighter of the free world from the late 1970s, with over 500 produced
